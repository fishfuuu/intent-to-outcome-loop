## Intentional forks (modified)

All three skills in this pack are **intentional forks**, not upstream
snapshots. Upstream names are retained. Repository copies use LF.

- `research-synthesis` — derived from
  `skills/research-synthesis/SKILL.md`
  Fork scope: position rule (strongest judgment the evidence supports, not a
  mandatory position), so-what that does not force a recommendation, a scope
  boundary against turning synthesis into a research program, host-neutral
  wording, and links limited to this pack.
- `decision-memo` — derived from `skills/decision-memo/SKILL.md`
  Fork scope: a decision position of RECOMMEND / DEFER / ESCALATE, "default
  to one page" instead of "one page, no exceptions", the memo preparing a
  human decision rather than owning it, host-neutral wording, and links
  limited to this pack.
- `reviewing-output` — derived from `skills/reviewing-output/SKILL.md`
  Fork scope: host-neutral wording, removal of the code-or-automation review
  pass plus an explicit engineering-review boundary, proportionality between
  material and trivial outputs, claim-versus-evidence discipline
  (UNVERIFIED vs FALSE), and links limited to this pack.
